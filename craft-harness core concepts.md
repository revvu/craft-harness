# Craft Harness — Core Concepts

This document distills the load-bearing ideas of the system.
`craft-harness plan.md` is the operational specification; this file is the conceptual foundation that should survive plan revisions.

## 1. The Governing Rule

> Human mastery grants agent capability.

The objective is not maximal agent capability.
It is maximal agent capability subject to the constraint that capability remains a legible amplification of the operator's own understanding, skill, judgment, and taste.

## 2. Two Gates, One Loop — the Responsibility Breakdown

The system is a division of epistemic labor with hard boundaries:

| Actor | Direction | World access | May do | May never do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Teacher agent** | world → operator | unrestricted | research materials, materialize frontier nodes and paths with citations, orchestrate learning, generate and grade assessments, recommend promotions | write mastery levels; teach from its own weights instead of cited materials |
| **Worker agent** | operator → world | denied by default | plan tasks, declare required units, execute what the checker authorizes, compose unlocked primitives novelly | act on undeclared or locked premises; browse/discover on the operator's behalf; silently patch gaps from pretraining |
| **Deterministic checker** | — | none | decide `required ⊆ unlocked` ; record authorizing unit IDs in execution traces | exercise judgment |
| **Operator** | — | — | approve sources, learn, sit assessments, review evidence, promote/revoke/split/merge, resolve unresolved path leaves | be bypassed |

Two gates:

- The **worker gate** keeps agent output inside the operator's mastery.
- The **teacher gate** keeps the operator's learning inside the established human canon.

The LLM supplies reasoning, orchestration, and assessment mechanics on both sides, but is never the source of record on either.

**Provenance chain:** agent output ← operator mastery ← evidence artifacts ← established human materials.
Every arrow has an explicit gate; nothing bottoms out in model weights.

## 3. The Three Pressures — How the Registry Grows

The knowledge space is never represented globally.
It has three regions — verified **interior**, materialized **frontier**, implicit **cloud** — and a unit leaves the cloud only under one of three pressures:

1. **Gaps** (demand): the worker's plan is blocked on a unit; building things reveals what you need.
2. **Aspirations** (pull): the operator names a distant goal; curiosity reveals where you want to go.
3. **Expansion** (supply): unlocking a unit materializes its curriculum-adjacent neighbors; mastery reveals what is nearby.

Because these are the only entry paths, the registry stays relevant by construction: it contains exactly the knowledge that your work demanded, your curiosity pulled, or your mastery touched.
There is no ontology committee and no upfront enumeration — the map is generated by living with the system.

Gaps and aspirations may name targets deep in the cloud; both resolve through the same path-finding routine (§5).

## 4. The Claim/Decision Split

The recurring structural pattern, everywhere in the system:

> **LLMs generate claims. Claims must be verifiable artifacts. Deterministic layers decide over claims.**

Instances:

| LLM produces (claim) | Deterministic layer decides | Human checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| worker plan with `requires:` lists | `required ⊆ unlocked` check | spot-checks of traces |
| prerequisite edge claims with citations | path search over the claim graph (§5) | source approval before learning |
| scope-match verdicts ("this prereq is covered by unit X") | recorded, versioned, applied | veto at path review |
| assessment grade against rubric | rubric was pre-registered before the attempt | promotion approval after viewing evidence |

An LLM output that is not a citable, checkable claim is inadmissible as an input to any gate.
This is what makes the system auditable end to end despite LLMs doing most of the cognitive work.

## 5. Path-Finding

**Problem.** Given a target unit T (from a gap or aspiration) and the covered set (unlocked ∪ claimed ∪ granted), produce a minimal prerequisite chain from the covered set to T, every node sourced in established curricula.

**Key structural fact (from the spikes):** the graph does not pre-exist.
Edges are claims extracted from real curricula by research.
So path-finding separates cleanly into **graph construction** (LLM as researcher, producing claim data) and **graph search** (deterministic algorithm over that data).

### 5.1 The algorithm

```text
1. TARGET NORMALIZATION            [LLM + research; operator confirms]
   Map the goal phrase to a scoped unit definition, scope-matched
   against source tables of contents (course titles lie).

2. PREREQUISITE ELICITATION        [LLM + research; per uncovered node]
   prereqs(U) → edge claims. Each claim carries:
     { prereq unit, target unit, kind, curriculum, source citation,
       verified_against URL, verification tier, revision date }
   Admissibility rule (deterministic): no verified citation → no edge.

3. COVERAGE MATCHING               [LLM verdict; operator may veto]
   For each discovered prereq: covered by an existing
   unlocked/claimed/granted unit, or new? Scope-match, not name-match.
   Verdicts are recorded claims, reviewed with the path.

4. BACKWARD SEARCH                 [deterministic]
   Expand uncovered nodes via steps 2–3 until every leaf is covered,
   or depth/node budget is hit. Unresolved leaves surface to the
   operator: mark GRANTED, mark CLAIMED, or authorize more research.

5. MINIMAL-CLOSURE SELECTION       [deterministic, exact]
   Prerequisites are AND-edges (a unit needs all prereqs in a set),
   but different curricula offer alternative prereq-sets (OR between
   claim groups). Compute:

     cost(U) = 0                          if U is covered
     cost(U) = min over alternative        otherwise
               prereq-sets S of
               [ effort(U) + Σ_{p ∈ S} cost(p) ]

   with memoization and a cycle guard. AND/OR minimization is hard in
   general but exact and instant at real sizes (depth ≲ 5, few
   alternatives per node).

6. PATH REVIEW                     [operator]
   The proposed chain, per-edge citations and coverage verdicts
   inline. Approving it doubles as the source-approval gate.
```

### 5.2 The cost function (deterministic, tunable)

```text
edge/unit cost = effort(U) × kind_penalty × confidence_penalty

kind_penalty:        stated-prerequisite  1.0
                     (catalog prereq, "this book assumes X")
                     sequence-position    ~3.0
                     (merely comes earlier in a course sequence)

confidence_penalty:  decreases with multi-curriculum corroboration
                     and verification tier; increases with age
                     (revision dates).

effort(U):           estimated from source size (chapters, stated
                     hours) — an LLM estimate, but recorded on the
                     node and operator-visible, never hidden.
```

The `kind_penalty` encodes the geometry lesson: school sequences interpose geometry between algebra 1 and statistics, but no stated prerequisite requires it.
Scheduling artifacts are dominated, not banned — if no stated-prerequisite route exists, sequence evidence still yields a path.

### 5.3 The determinism ledger

Deterministic (pure algorithm over stored claims):

- edge admissibility (citation required), staleness checks
- backward search, budgets, termination
- minimal-closure selection and the cost function
- coverage bookkeeping; trace recording

LLM-as-researcher (output = cited claims, verified by fetch):

- target normalization; prerequisite elicitation; edge-kind labeling; effort estimation

LLM-as-judge (output = recorded verdicts, human-vetoable):

- coverage/scope matching

Operator:

- confirm target scope; approve the path/sources; resolve unresolved leaves; veto matches

Properties this buys:

- **Reproducibility**: same claim store + same weights → same path. Claims persist; re-running search is replayable.
- **Auditability**: every hop in a chain traces to a citation and a verdict, both stored.
- **Cheap self-correction**: research nondeterminism is confined to claim generation, where verification, corroboration, and revision dates catch drift (e.g., a prerequisite a curriculum dropped last year).

## 6. Claims and Verify-on-First-Use

The interior is lazy: prior mastery enters as `CLAIMED` — an operator assertion with no evidence.

- Claims **anchor path-finding**: the covered set includes claimed units, so chains never force re-learning what you already know.
- Claims **grant nothing at the worker gate**: the checker treats CLAIMED as locked.
- The first time the gate actually demands a claimed unit, an **express assessment** (minutes, not hours) converts it to UNLOCKED.

This resolves bootstrapping: no day-one enumeration marathon, and no unverified claims silently powering the worker.
Claims shape the map immediately; capability is granted only at the moment it matters, and cheaply.

**Worked example.**
The operator names an aspiration: "statistical inference."
The path-finder researches backward and discovers the chain presupposes intermediate algebra, which presupposes algebra 1 — which the system has never heard of the operator knowing.
Without CLAIMED, the system would demand an algebra 1 assessment before the statistics journey even begins.
Instead the operator marks `math.algebra-1: CLAIMED` — one line, no assessment — and the path-finder treats it as covered, so the chain correctly starts at intermediate algebra.
Months later, in an unrelated context, a worker plan includes a step requiring `math.algebra-1` (solving a small linear system in a pricing script).
The checker sees the unit is only CLAIMED, so it triggers an express assessment: a couple of fresh problems, ten minutes, assistant-free.
Pass → UNLOCKED with real evidence attached → the worker proceeds.
The claim did two different jobs at two different times: it shaped the map immediately, and it deferred verification to the exact moment capability was actually demanded.

## 7. Constructive Assessment

> Prefer "build a thing that performs" over "answer questions about the thing."

Example: to certify `ai.classical-search` (pathfinding, game-tree search, heuristics), the assessment is not a quiz — it is: **build an Othello player that beats a provided baseline player**, assistant-free, in a logged session.

Why this is the preferred assessment shape wherever the domain allows it:

- **Performance-graded**: winning against a fixed baseline is objective and hard to fake; grading barely involves judgment.
- **Integrative**: one artifact exercises a cluster of sub-concepts at once — evidence fans in (one artifact can certify several units), matching the many-to-many unit↔source reality.
- **Pre-registration comes free**: the baseline opponent and acceptance criteria are fixed before the attempt — they *are* the rubric.
- **Engaging**: assessments feel like projects, not exams; the evidence artifact (the code, the match log) is durable and worth keeping.

Mapping to the mastery ladder: L3's "proctored cold solve" generalizes to "cold-build against a fixed baseline"; L4's transfer becomes "beat a changed variant" (bigger board, different rules) or "critique/repair a flawed player."

Declarative domains get the constructive analog: for philosophy, "apply framework X to this novel case and defend it against three pre-registered objections" rather than definition recall.

Assessment design is flagged as a major workstream that will undergo significant further thought; this section fixes only the direction of preference.

## 8. Organic Frontier Fan-Out

Expansion is automatic on unlock, but fan-out is a **score threshold, not a fixed top-k**.

Candidate neighbors are scored by:

- source support (verified citations; multi-curriculum corroboration)
- relevance to active goals and aspirations
- edge-type diversity (don't surface four deepenings; mix mechanism, generalization, lateral moves)

Candidates above threshold materialize — typically around four, organically more when a unit sits at a genuine crossroads and fewer at a dead end.

## 9. Half-Life Honesty

Evidence is timestamped and decays.
A unit the operator hasn't personally exercised within its half-life goes stale: flagged for re-probe, treated as reference-only by the gate.
Agent use of a skill does not count as operator practice — delegation accelerates decay, and the system surfaces that truth instead of hiding it.

## 10. The System in Motion

The whole design in one sentence:

> A lazily-growing, evidence-backed map of the operator's actual competence, with a worker that can only act inside the map, and a teacher that can only extend the map along paths that verifiably exist in the human canon.

A representative week:

1. The operator gives the worker a task; it plans, declares what each step requires, and the checker blocks on one unit — the task parks and a gap-goal materializes.
2. The teacher path-finds from the interior to that goal using cited curricula, often finding shorter chains than intuition suggests.
3. The operator approves the path and its sources, learns from them, sits a proctored constructive assessment against a pre-registered baseline, reviews the evidence file, and approves the promotion.
4. The parked task resumes; the newly unlocked unit sprouts its highest-scoring neighbors as frontier options.
5. Separately, the operator drops an aspiration anywhere in the cloud, and the same path-finder builds a minimal chain to it.
6. Months later, delegated-but-untouched units go stale on their half-life and drop to reference-only until a quick re-probe.

The registry is generated by exactly three pressures — what you build, what you're curious about, and what's nearby — so it stays relevant by construction, with no upfront ontology and no enumeration of what you already know.
