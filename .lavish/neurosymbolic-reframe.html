v2 · pass 1 supersedes the agent-harness framing

Craft Harness

A neurosymbolic representation of your understanding — a medium that makes the ideas, concepts, and skills you provably know easily leverageable and composable.

Not an agent wrapper. Not gating. Not permissions. A personal standard library for cognition.

1 · The inversion

Compose up, don't filter down

The decision that reset the architecture: stop subtracting from an omniscient agent, start composing from your proven primitives.

v1 — Filter a general intelligence down

superseded
  • • An agent does the work; a checker + auditor subtract what you haven't earned
  • • Permission lists, gated execution, adversarial audits
  • • Leakage = breach of delegated authority → high stakes, heavy machinery
Why it died (spike-proven): the worker parks what it knows it lacks, and smuggles what it doesn't know it's using. Subtraction leaks, and leaky subtraction needs police — the auditor. Policing is constitutive of the gating frame, not incidental.

v2 — Compose a personal intelligence up

current
  • • The registry is the substance the system is built from, not a filter over a model
  • • Nothing needs denying — nothing outside your space is offered
  • • You are the executor; the system is the medium → residual leakage is an autocomplete-quality issue, not a breach
The auditor dissolves — not because integrity got lazy, but because the architecture no longer has the hole the auditor was patching.

2 · The two layers

Where "neurosymbolic" is literal

Symbolic — the registry graph

  • • Concept/skill nodes with concrete realizations — your stacks, idioms, actual past code
  • • Composition edges: what a node is built from, what it enables
  • • Provenance (the real work that grounds it) + freshness (last personal use)
  • • Deterministic binding resolution — same intent, same expansion

Neural — the LLM, bound

  • • Intent understanding: fuzzy expression → registry terms
  • • Expansion within bindings, in your idioms, seeded from your artifacts
  • • Autocomplete graduated by assistance tier
  • • Librarian routing — sources, never synthesis
The discipline between the layers (survives from v1): the neural layer proposes bindings and extractions; the symbolic layer records them; resolution thereafter is deterministic and auditable.

7 · Survival map

What survives, transforms, dissolves

Survives as-is

  • • lazy materialization (interior / frontier / cloud)
  • • three pressures on growth
  • • pathfinding w/ verified citations, typed edges
  • • claim/decision split
  • • no-distillation principle
  • • granularity by demand, split/merge

Transforms

  • • mastery ladder → assistance tiers
  • • capability gap → hole / undefined symbol
  • • teacher agent → librarian
  • • half-life → freshness w/ gentle tier decay
  • • CLAIMED / verify-on-first-use → provenance mining
  • • positive grounding → the entire system

Dissolves

  • • worker agent as executor
  • • deterministic permission checker as spine
  • • the LLM auditor
  • • proctored exams as evidence spine (demoted to optional instrument)
The explicit trade: v2 gives up the autonomous worker — no agent doing tasks unattended inside your mastery. Leverage now means a radically better medium for your execution. An agent layer can return later on top of the compositional substrate — a far sounder foundation than gating.
